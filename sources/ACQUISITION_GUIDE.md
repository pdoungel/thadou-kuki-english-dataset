# Thadou-Kuki Data Acquisition Guide

This guide records the next acquisition targets for expanding the corpus without silently importing copyrighted material.

## P0 — authorized acquisition

### LDC-IL Mother Tongue Parallel Text Corpus of India Vol. I

The official LDC-IL catalogue lists **Thado/Thadou** as a language component. Acquire the component through the authorized access route and record its exact redistribution and AI/model-training terms before ingestion.

### Bharatavani Thadou resources

Bharatavani's Thadou language-learning area lists works including *Thaadou Phonetic Reader*, *Adult Primer in Kuki*, and *Grammar of Thadou-Kuki: A Descriptive Study*. Its Thadou dictionaries area is a potential terminology source.

Website accessibility is not treated as a blanket corpus license. Rights must be checked for the individual work/content partner.

## P1 — education and linguistic references

COHSEM publishes Thadou-Kuki curriculum/examination documents. These are useful for identifying contemporary vocabulary, grammar topics and textbooks. Use them as discovery/reference material unless reuse permission is established.

The Directorate of Language Planning and Implementation, Manipur has documented work on standard grammars for Thadou-Kuki and other tribal languages. Locate the completed grammar and determine its access and reuse terms.

The UC eScholarship record for Pauthang Haokip's *Word formation in Thadou* is useful for designing morphological annotation and normalization. The article remains copyrighted unless its record explicitly grants a reuse license.

## P2 — specialized registers

The 2024 study *The dying ritual language of Thadou-Kuki* documents 13 ritual texts and is useful for designing a traditional/ritual register category. Treat the article and its examples as copyrighted research material unless permission says otherwise.

WHDL catalogs a Thadou-language Church of the Nazarene manual with free PDF access. Free download does not by itself establish corpus redistribution or model-training permission; inspect item-level terms before extraction.

## Acquisition record

For every acquired source, record:

- source_id
- exact title
- author/editor
- publisher/content partner
- year
- source URL
- retrieval date
- file checksum
- language/variety
- data type/register
- license
- redistribution permission
- model-training permission
- attribution requirements
- whether raw text may enter the public repository
- notes on OCR/manual transcription quality

Only after these fields are resolved should a source move from metadata-only to an included public corpus.
